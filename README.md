## 🔬 Current Research: RealBench

I am a **co-first author of RealBench**, an open-source benchmark that examines how AI weather models perform under operational conditions and extreme weather challenges.

**RealBench: Benchmarking Data-Driven Numerical Weather Forecasting Under Operational Conditions and Extreme Event Challenges**

**Ruize Li**<sup>*</sup>, Zhibin Wen<sup>*</sup>, Tao Han<sup>*</sup>, Hao Chen, Fenghua Ling, Wei Zhang, Song Guo, Lei Bai

*Equal contribution by the first three authors.*

arXiv preprint, 2026. Submitted to NeurIPS 2026, Evaluations and Datasets Track; under review.

- Deployed distributed inference and evaluation on GPU clusters, with dedicated assessments for **heatwaves, cold waves, and tropical cyclones**.
