## 🔬 Selected Research

### RealBench

**RealBench: Benchmarking Data-Driven Medium-Range Weather Forecasting Under Operational Conditions and Extreme Event Challenges**

**Ruize Li**<sup>*</sup>, Zhibin Wen<sup>*</sup>, Tao Han<sup>*</sup>, Hao Chen, Fenghua Ling, Wei Zhang, Song Guo, Lei Bai  
<sup>*</sup> Equal contribution.

**arXiv:2605.24945 · Under Review**

<p align="left">
  <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24945">

A large-scale benchmark for evaluating neural forecasting systems under **temporal distribution shift, operational initialization, observation-based verification, and extreme events**.

- Designed a strictly **time-out-of-distribution evaluation protocol** using 2025 data.
- Built large-scale inference and evaluation pipelines for **Pangu-Weather, GraphCast, Aurora, AIFS, and ECMWF IFS**.
- Integrated operational analyses, reanalysis, and observations from **10,000+ weather stations**.
- Evaluated model reliability on **heatwaves, cold surges, and tropical cyclones** using distributed GPU workflows.

This work motivated my broader interest in **long-horizon modeling and generative modeling of complex spatiotemporal systems**.

### Mathematical Contest in Modeling (MCM) 2026
**Team Leader · Meritorious Winner (Top ~7% Globally)**

Developed a closed-loop dynamical modeling framework for a continuous physical system.

- Coupled nonlinear **ODE-based state evolution** with empirical drivers and solved the system using adaptive **RK45** integration.
- Implemented an **Extended Kalman Filter (EKF)** to fuse noisy observations with model predictions, achieving **R² = 0.936** in state estimation.
- Performed **uncertainty quantification** using Monte Carlo simulation and parameter sensitivity analysis.
- Led the modeling, numerical implementation, analysis, and technical presentation.

### VQ-Prompt in LibContinual
**Continual Learning · Vision Transformers · Prompt Learning**

Reproduced and adapted VQ-Prompt under the LibContinual continual-learning framework.

- Adapted a **Vision Transformer (ViT)** backbone and prompt-learning modules to LibContinual.
- Studied architectural differences between **CODA-Prompt and VQ-Prompt**, especially their attention and prompt mechanisms.
- Modified attention components for framework compatibility.
- Reproduced performance within approximately **2–4% of the reported baseline**.

### Contextual Lipschitz Bandits
**Research Intern · Advised by Prof. Lin Yang**

Studied sequential decision-making in continuous context spaces and developed a local data aggregation mechanism that shares observations across similar contexts.

### Climate & Spatiotemporal Data Analysis
**Research Student · Advised by Prof. Sandro M. Ferreira Veiga**

Applied **Empirical Orthogonal Function (EOF)** analysis to large-scale climate datasets to study North Pacific SST variability and East Asian summer rainfall.
