**RealBench: Benchmarking Data-Driven Medium-Range Weather Forecasting Under Operational Conditions and Extreme Event Challenges**

RealBench studies how modern data-driven forecasting systems behave under **temporal distribution shift, operational initialization, observation-based verification, and extreme events**.

My work included:

- Designing a strictly **time-out-of-distribution evaluation protocol** using 2025 data for modern neural forecasting systems.
- Building evaluation pipelines for **Pangu-Weather, GraphCast, Aurora, AIFS, and ECMWF IFS**, covering both standard forecasting metrics and extreme-event evaluation.
- Integrating operational analyses, reanalysis products, and observations from **10,000+ weather stations** to study gaps between benchmark performance and real-world forecasting.
- Developing and deploying large-scale inference and evaluation workflows on GPU clusters.
- Studying reliability under **heatwaves, cold surges, and tropical cyclones**, with a broader interest in distribution shift and long-horizon model behavior.

This project led me to broader questions about **spatiotemporal generative modeling, long-horizon prediction, uncertainty, multimodal observations, and reliable evaluation of foundation models**.
